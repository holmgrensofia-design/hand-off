# Fireplace recording

`dist/assets/fireplace-recorded.mp3` derives from “Fireplace”, by Freesound contributor martats, published 2011, CC0. Source: https://sonicdictionary.duke.edu/items/fireplace.html

Original WAV: https://sonicdictionary.duke.edu/sites/sonicdictionary.duke.edu/files/site-files/55459047b553625889f54f6d8795e85b.wav

Converted to MP3 with short edge fades for looping. Optional playback; stops when the room closes, the page is hidden, or sound is muted. No rain sound.
