# Current product and design brief

## Established prototype
- Exactly 30 days. Steps are the main challenge; purple UI, circular calendar and small add-on dots are liked.
- Manual step entry and nightly lantern ritual stay. Tap and steady hold-to-adjust controls exist. Thirty-day goal = daily reference ×30; show both.
- Every deliberately marked day advances the garden, including below-reference days. Partial lantern glow shows that day's proportion. Changing the reference must not extinguish previous lanterns or rewrite their saved reference.
- Optional add-ons are lighter, never a second gate to the main reveal. Stretch and breakfast are implemented; other ideas are not automatically features.
- The press-and-hold ritual is silent. Extras have a tiny optional tick. Preserve the cosy room's fireplace recording and audio credit. No new opening chime.
- Keep the original painting, palette and softness. Tiny details are intentional. Approved: robin, owl and two owlets, grounded hedgehog above nearest lantern, pumpkins and snail family. Do not enlarge them merely to increase measured pixel change.
- The rejected mushrooms are removed. Day 13 reveals the nearest painted lantern; it stays unlit through Day 12. The other lantern timings remain as in the baseline.
- No text over the main garden. Golden-key secret is separate from finishing Day 30. Real secret thresholds and accessibility are still open.
- Preserve seasonal/date ideas for later; only one autumn landscape is developed. Main current effort is making that experience work, not adding twelve worlds now.

## Parked ideas, not instructions to build
- Weather selection during lighting, with lantern/scene weather and a month-long weather diary.
- Preset optional habits plus a possible custom add-on; French speaking, no coffee, stretching, strength and breakfast are ideas. No French conversation engine is built.
- Future landscapes: clear, detailed resting patches planned into the ORIGINAL art (moss, stone, roots, branches), with softer atmosphere elsewhere. Candles, lanterns, glowing windows, stars and moonlight are especially loved.
- No coins or credits. Completion reward is the unfolding landscape, like an advent calendar or puzzle.
- Monetization is undecided: one full free challenge once; annual pass around 199 SEK versus purchased reusable seasonal collections plus optional themed packs. Paid-landscape reuse matters to Sofia; subscription ownership/access semantics remain unresolved. Do not implement paywalls, purchases or a final price yet.
